---
description: Why is Streamer.bot crashing on Linux due to UIAutomationCore.dll?
---

If you are using Streamer.bot v1.0.5 or later and experiencing crashes due to `UIAutomationCore.dll`, please follow the instructions in the [UIAutomationCore Fix](https://docs.streamer.bot/get-started/installation/linux/uiautomationcore) guide.
